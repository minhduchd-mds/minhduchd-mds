# Repository Lifecycle Policy

## Flagship

Strategic product with active architecture, tests, documentation and a differentiated product thesis. Must satisfy the strongest quality gates.

## Active

Maintained product or modernization target with a clear owner and roadmap. May still contain legacy debt but cannot be directionless.

## Incubator

Promising experiment with a specific hypothesis and review date. If it does not graduate, merge useful work into a flagship or move to history.

## History

Learning/demo work kept for provenance or portfolio history. No agent infrastructure, dependency churn or production claims.

## Archive

Obsolete, duplicated or superseded repository. Keep read-only where history matters.

## Promotion criteria

A repository moves upward only when it has: a clear About statement, differentiated capability, repeatable build/test path, architecture boundary, roadmap and documented provenance.
